## Featured Projects

### 1. Mechanical Line-Climber (Capstone)
* **The Challenge:** Ascending a vertical tether under strict mass, power, and traction constraints.
* **Technical Contribution:** Designed the high-reduction gear train and custom dual-pulley clamping mechanism. Modeled the required stall torque in MATLAB to size the brushless motor under dynamic load conditions.

### 2. Aircraft Trajectory Simulation & Optimization
* **Focus:** Point-mass aircraft trajectory algorithms and supersonic performance envelope modeling.
* **Implementation:** Built numerical simulation pipelines using Python and PyTorch to evaluate trajectory states, compute aerodynamic drag profiles, and optimize climb profiles under airspace constraints.
